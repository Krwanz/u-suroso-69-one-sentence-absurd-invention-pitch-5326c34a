## The pitch

> A self-inflating whoopee cushion for office chairs that apologises in a posh accent every time you sit down too hard, so your colleagues blame the furniture, not you.

One sentence, 166 characters: a ridiculous invention that fixes the tiny everyday annoyance of an embarrassing noise when you sit down too hard at work — by making the chair itself take the blame, politely.

## Why this one

- **Funny:** the laugh is in the invention itself — a whoopee cushion with manners, apologising in a posh accent.
- **Clever:** it solves the embarrassment not by silencing the noise but by redirecting the blame to the furniture.
- **Original:** no alarm clock, no sock, no kettle — a self-inflating, self-apologising office chair cushion.
